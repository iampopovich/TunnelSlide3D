<p>TunnelSlide3D is an open-source remake of the classic tunnel arcade SpeedX 3D. You race down an endless neon tunnel that keeps speeding up; tilt the phone to slide around the walls and dodge the blocks.</p>
<p>The original was abandoned years ago and its last builds are flagged by antivirus software because of ad SDKs. This remake recreates the rules and the feel from scratch: no ads, no trackers, no network access.</p>
<p><b>What is inside</b></p>
<ul>
<li>The tunnel opens into a flat plane or turns inside out while you ride; you can fall off the edge of the plane.</li>
<li>Obstacle patterns: walls, gates, spirals, wall-to-wall pillars, cubes that roll on a beat.</li>
<li>Bonuses: shields, quake, time dilation, space fold.</li>
<li>Hazard zones: blackout, dark nebula, hue shift, gravity well, plasma cloud.</li>
<li>Five difficulty levels with a separate best score each, tilt calibration.</li>
<li>Classic style with heavy motion blur, red/cyan and green/magenta 3D glasses, monochrome mode.</li>
<li>Procedural music: a calm generative piano, or a fast beat that follows your speed.</li>
</ul>
